In 2012, a CT scan revealed what embalmers had hidden for 3,000 years: a deep cut across the throat of Pharaoh Ramesses III. The last great warrior king of Egypt had been murdered, and the trial of his killers still survives on papyrus.

A royal wife who wanted her son on the throne. Palace officials, smuggled messages, a call to rebellion, and wax figures made with magic from the king's own library. A trial where even the judges were corrupted. And a mysterious "screaming mummy" that DNA finally identified as the king's son.

This is the Harem Conspiracy, told as an evidence board: every name, letter and verdict from the records, and the forensic evidence that closed a 3,000-year-old cold case.
