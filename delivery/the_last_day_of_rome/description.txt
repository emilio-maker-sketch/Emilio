On 29 May 1453, after a siege of fifty-three days, Constantinople fell to the army of the 21-year-old Ottoman sultan Mehmed II. With it ended the Roman Empire, a state that traced an unbroken history back almost fifteen centuries to Augustus.

This documentary follows the siege from the building of the fortress that cut the Bosphorus to the final assault, and tells you where the sources agree, where they disagree, and which famous stories rest on a single chronicler.
