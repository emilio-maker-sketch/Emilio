## Upload checklist
- **Altered or synthetic content:** this label is meant for realistic footage or voices that could be mistaken for real people or events. An AI narrator that doesn't imitate a real person generally doesn't need it, and the description and end card already disclose the AI voice. If you're unsure, ticking **Yes** does no harm.
- **Music:** Kevin MacLeod tracks are CC BY 4.0. Keep the credit lines in the description.
- **Chapters:** they appear automatically from the timestamps in the description.
