On the Ides of March, 44 BC, Julius Caesar was stabbed to death at a meeting of the Senate by men he had pardoned, promoted and trusted. They believed they were saving the Roman Republic. Within twenty years it was gone.

This documentary follows the road to the Ides, from the bloody century before Caesar to the Rubicon, the civil war and the dictatorship, through the conspiracy and the murder itself, to the funeral, the rise of Octavian and the end at Philippi. Along the way it separates what the ancient sources actually say from the legends: no 'Et tu, Brute?', no murder on the Capitol.
