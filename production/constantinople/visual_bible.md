# Visual bible

## Look
A high-budget historical reconstruction: real light, real textures, real weight. The camera always moves slowly and with purpose, never with a jittery "AI zoom". Every shot illustrates the sentence it sits under.

**Lens and texture:** anamorphic 2.39-feel framing inside 16:9, gentle film grain, soft halation on highlights, deep but detailed shadows. No HDR glow, no oversaturation, no plastic skin.

## Colour arc
The grade tells the story. It starts warm and goes cold as the city's chances run out.

| Grade key | Used for | Description |
|---|---|---|
| `gold` | Ch 1–4, the city's past, Mehmed's triumph, the walls today | Golden-hour sun, amber and honey, haze over water |
| `candle` | Interiors, Hagia Sophia, councils, scribes | Candle and oil-lamp light, gold mosaic glints, incense haze |
| `steel` | The siege by day | Overcast cold daylight, steel-blue and stone-grey, gunpowder smoke |
| `night` | Night attacks, the last night | Moonlight blue against orange firelight and torches |
| `dawn` | 29 May | Pre-dawn cold blue to pale grey, fires on the horizon |
| `ash` | Aftermath | Muted, low contrast, ash-grey and faded ochre |

## Shot grammar
- Each paragraph opens wide (establish), moves to medium (action), and lands on a close-up (meaning).
- No static image holds longer than ~7 s. Stills always carry a move (push, pull, lateral parallax) plus living atmosphere: smoke, dust, candle flicker.
- Transitions: a straight cut inside a sequence, a slow dissolve between places, a match cut on shape or light between eras (Augustus' legion → Byzantine defenders), and a cut to black only at three moments (after the census, after "the city is taken", before the last chapter).
- Slow motion only twice: the great bombard's first shot, and Constantine dropping his cloak.

## Things that must never appear
Minarets on Hagia Sophia before the conquest · the Blue Mosque, Topkapı, Galata Bridge or Süleymaniye in 1453 · fezzes · plate-armoured "knights" on the Ottoman side · gore · text or watermarks inside generated images · fantasy armour.
