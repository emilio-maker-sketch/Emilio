## Judgement
1. **How the Great Pyramid Was Built** — recommended.
   - Strongest signals together: rising search (+84.8%), a 201× long-form outlier this month at our length (50 min), and moderate competition (50–54).
   - Same "how was it built" promise as Derinkuyu, so the two films support each other.
   - Real evidence is unusually rich and documented: the Wadi al-Jarf papyri (the diary of the inspector Merer, c. 2560 BC), the workers' town at Giza, quarries, ramps, the copper-tool debate. Real photos are plentiful on Wikimedia Commons.
   - Risk: a crowded subject full of fringe claims. The film wins by being the evidence-first version (Merer's diary is the hook).
2. **The Herculaneum scrolls** — second. Lowest competition (40) at ~98K searches, a great story (carbonised library now being read with AI), follows our Pompeii film. Less trend momentum than the pyramids today.
3. **Cleopatra's lost tomb** — not recommended now: search down 57.5%, three competing videos this month, and no tomb has been found, so the film would rest on speculation.
