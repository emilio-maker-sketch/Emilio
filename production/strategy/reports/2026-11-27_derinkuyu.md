## Judgement and plan
- Retention: our videos lose half the audience by about 70 seconds. The cold open must say within 15 seconds what the film is: a city dug straight down into the rock under a Turkish town, and the three questions it answers (how, who, why).
- The accurate angle is the gap: no ranked English film separates the evidence from the myths (18 levels, 20,000 people, Xenophon, giants and aliens). The last chapter does that openly.
- Length: about 45–50 minutes, real photographs and drawn graphics only. No AI images.

## Risks
- Key primary sources (Aydan & Ulusay 2013; the Turkish Ministry of Culture pages) are blocked from this environment. Any figure that only they would confirm stays off screen.
- Every photo needs a by-eye check against its title. Kaymaklı and Özkonak photos must be labelled as such and not passed off as Derinkuyu.
